# How Harimon speaks

The shop's look is marble and timber: a butcher's counter in daylight. The words match it — quiet, exact, confident.

## Principles

- **Facts sell, adjectives don't.** "Aged 28 days · 3 cm" beats "exceptional quality". Days aged, thickness, core
  temperature, where on the animal — these are the luxury.
- **Short sentences. Second person plural in Hebrew (אתם).** Talk like the butcher across the counter, not like an ad.
- **Say what happens, including the uncomfortable part.** The hold on the card, the real weight, what is out of
  stock and when it returns.
- **Kashrut is stated plainly and correctly.** Meat is sold kosher-salted; liver needs broiling (צלייה) before
  cooking; recipes never include dairy.

## Banned

מפנק · חוויה קולינרית · טעם של פעם · איכות ללא פשרות · הכי טוב · "!" in system copy · marketing superlatives in
English ("unbeatable", "mouth-watering").

## Mechanics

- Hebrew punctuation: ק״ג, ס״מ, גר׳, דק׳ (gershayim ״ and geresh ׳, not quotes).
- Temperatures in °C, weights in grams and kilograms, prices in shekels with the ₪ sign after the number.
- English mirrors the meaning idiomatically — never a word-for-word translation.
- Buttons say what happens: "הוספה לסל", "לסל", "למדריך המלא".
